Frequently Asked Questions

The Employment Permit System (EPS) in South Korea is a government initiative designed to manage the employment of foreign workers in sectors where there is a labor shortage. Under this system, employers can legally hire foreign workers through a structured process that includes the following key elements:
Eligibility, Application Process, Recruitment, Employment Conditions, Duration and Renewal and Support Services.

The EPS-TOPIK (Employment Permit System - Test of Proficiency in Korean) is a Korean language proficiency test specifically designed for foreign workers who want to work in South Korea under the Employment Permit System (EPS).

The EPS-TOPIK exam is conducted in various countries, primarily in Southeast Asia and South Asia. Here are some of the countries where the EPS-TOPIK exam is conducted:
Laos, Cambodia, Philippines, Mongolia, East Timor, Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Myanmar, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan.

To work in South Korea under the Employment Permit System (EPS), you generally need to meet the following requirements:
Basic Requirements:
  • Pass the EPS-TOPIK: The EPS-TOPIK is a test administered by the Human Resources Development Service of Korea (HRD) that assesses your basic communication skills, knowledge of industrial safety, and understanding of Korean culture. You must pass the test to be eligible for employment in Korea.
  • Age: Between 18 and 40 years old.
  • Health: Medically fit to work according to Korean government standards.
  • Have no criminal record:You must not have a criminal record.
  • Deportation History:No previous record of deportation or eviction from Korea.
  • Passport:Valid passport.
For the most accurate and up-to-date information, please refer to the official website of the Human Resources Development Service of Korea (HRD Korea)

The Employment Permit System (EPS) selection process for foreign workers in Korea includes the following steps:
EPS-TOPIK: The Human Resources Development Service of Korea (HRD) administers the EPS-TOPIK (Test of Proficiency in Korean) to select migrant workers. The test evaluates basic communication skills, knowledge of Korean business culture, and industrial safety. To pass, candidates must score at least 80 out of 200 points(case to case basis). The score is valid for two years.
Skills test: First-round passers of the EPS-TOPIK must take a skills test. The test is automatically applied to first-round passers.
Employer selection: Candidates who pass the EPS-TOPIK can apply for jobs through sending agencies in their home countries.
Employment contract: Employers can work with employment centers to find foreign workers. Once a contract is signed, the candidate must complete preliminary training in their home country.
Pre- and post-admission training: Employer associations conduct pre-admission training, and post-admission training covers language, culture, immigration, and labor laws.
The EPS allows foreigners to work in Korea for up to three years, but contracts can be renewed for up to four years and 10 months.
